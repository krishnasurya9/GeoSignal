# AAMCT 2026 template check

Checked on 2026-10-05.

## Finding

No official downloadable LaTeX, Overleaf, Word, or ZIP paper template was linked from the official conference home page or submissions page during this check. The submissions page refers to a “prescribed conference template” for accepted abstracts but does not expose the file or detailed layout specification. This folder therefore contains no substitute or guessed template.

## Requirements actually stated on the official site

- Abstract: 250–500 words; state objectives, methods, key results, and significance, and align with a conference theme.
- Abstract screening: Technical Programme Committee review for relevance, originality, and scientific merit.
- Full paper: accepted abstracts are invited to submit a full-length paper using the prescribed template. Subject experts review the full paper for comprehensive findings, originality, technical soundness, proper references, and clear figures/tables. The site does not state whether this review is blind.
- Originality: work should be original/unpublished and not published or under consideration elsewhere.
- Dates shown: abstract submission deadline 31 October 2026; full-paper deadline 15 November 2026; conference 17–20 December 2026.
- Date conflict: the submissions page gives abstract acceptance notification as 5 November 2026 in its Important Dates schedule and elsewhere gives 15 October 2026. Confirm this with the organizers.
- Submission portal: Google Forms, linked from the official submissions page: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeaufmqYcu_PDCOj1bheGf1B-ZIjIpFf8Bqgxyitr5f4yMOKA/viewform?embedded=true
- Publication: the home page says papers are considered for conference proceedings and that selected papers may be considered for named journals. It does not identify a proceedings publisher/series, promise indexing, or provide a publication guarantee.
- Not specified on the pages checked: page limit, columns, font, margins, citation/reference style, keyword count, copyright form, originality declaration form, or a template download.
